== 3. the transmitter's leakage at the receiver input (PA output 7.1 dBm) ==
a TDD link never sees it (the receiver is off while the transmitter is on); a radar and a full-duplex link do
a receiver has two input compression points and the leakage meets one of them: a full-duplex link's leakage sits at the carrier, in band; an FMCW radar's beats to below the baseband high-pass and meets only the stages before it (out of band)
